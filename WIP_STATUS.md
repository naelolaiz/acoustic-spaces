# Work in progress: prototype of fitted walls on the 7-point wave grid

Not for merging. A research prototype, saved as it stands on 2026-11-11.

## What is here
A prototype that fits walls between the cells of the 7-point grid (cut cells) instead of placing
them on the nearest cell face. It was written to measure what fitted walls gain, not as a
feature: no scene setting, tests or docs.

## Next
Whether fitted walls are built as an off-by-default option, only documented, or researched further
is an open choice. If built: a scene setting, tests against the box modes and image sources,
docs and changelog.
